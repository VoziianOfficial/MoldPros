Privacy Policy

This page explains how MoldPros may collect, use, and protect information submitted through this independent mold remediation provider matching platform.

Independent platform notice

MoldPros helps homeowners connect with independent local mold remediation providers. MoldPros does not perform mold inspection, testing, remediation, removal, cleanup, restoration, consultation, or contracting work directly.

Privacy Policy Cookie Policy Terms of Service

1. Information We May Collect

When you submit a form on this website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, selected mold service category, affected area details, moisture history, timing, property notes, and other information you choose to provide.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to process your request, connect you with relevant independent mold remediation providers or provider networks, respond to inquiries, improve site functionality, support form operation, and maintain basic business records.

3. Sharing With Independent Providers

By submitting a request, you understand that your information may be shared with independent local providers or partner networks that may contact you regarding mold inspection, testing, remediation, black mold removal, attic or crawl space mold cleanup, or related property service requests.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve browsing, support form functionality, remember privacy preferences, and understand general site activity. You can review more details on the Cookie Policy page.

5. No Contractor, Medical, or Outcome Guarantee

MoldPros is not responsible for the work, pricing, warranties, licensing, insurance, availability, testing procedures, remediation methods, representations, or final results of independent providers. MoldPros does not provide medical advice, health evaluations, health guarantees, or guaranteed mold removal outcomes. Homeowners should verify all provider details before hiring.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to help protect submitted information. No website, form, email, or online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit a form through this website. If you do submit information, you may contact us to ask privacy-related questions or request that we review information associated with your inquiry, subject to applicable law and operational limitations.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@moldpros.com.
