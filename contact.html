Quick answers

Before the first request.

A few simple reminders keep the experience clear, safe, and transparent before you compare provider options.

Does MoldPros send a crew?

No. MoldPros is an independent matching platform and does not perform mold inspection, testing, remediation, removal, cleanup, restoration, or contracting work directly.

Can I compare more than one category?

Yes. Choose the closest category first, then add extra property context in the project note so provider options are easier to compare.

What should I verify before hiring?

Confirm license, insurance, written estimate, testing process, containment method, remediation scope, payment terms, project timeline, warranty details, and final pricing directly.
