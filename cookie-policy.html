1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, including browser local storage, may also be used for basic site functionality and preference storage.

2. How MoldPros May Use Cookies

MoldPros may use cookies or similar technologies to remember privacy choices, support form behavior, improve browsing experience, understand general site activity, and maintain basic website functionality.

3. Cookie Preference Banner

When you first visit this website, a privacy preference banner may appear. You may accept or decline the notice. The banner remains visible until a choice is made, unless a previous choice is already stored in your browser.

4. Local Storage

This website may use browser local storage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice. This helps avoid repeatedly showing the same banner on future visits from the same browser and device.

5. Form and Request Functionality

Cookies or similar technologies may support form interactions, message display, validation behavior, and basic user experience features related to mold remediation provider matching requests.

6. Third-Party Tools

Some embedded features, such as maps, fonts, icons, analytics tools, or other external resources, may be provided by third parties. Those services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies according to their own policies.

7. Managing Cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or local storage features may affect how certain parts of the website function, including preference storage or embedded tools.

8. No Contractor, Medical, or Outcome Guarantee

MoldPros is an independent matching platform. MoldPros does not perform mold inspection, testing, remediation, removal, cleanup, restoration, consultation, or contracting work directly. MoldPros does not provide medical advice, health evaluations, or guaranteed mold removal outcomes.
