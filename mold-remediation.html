Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting a mold remediation estimate.

Mold remediation projects can include containment, material handling, cleaning, disposal, moisture questions, repair coordination, and post-remediation expectations. These questions help make provider responses easier to compare.

What exactly is included in the remediation scope?

Ask whether the estimate includes containment, equipment, removal, cleaning, disposal, affected material handling, protection, cleanup, and post-remediation recommendations.

How will the affected area be contained?

Confirm how the provider plans to separate the work area, control dust or debris, protect surrounding spaces, and limit cross-contamination risk.

Will moisture source correction be included?

Ask whether leak repair, humidity control, ventilation changes, drainage issues, or other moisture source work is included, excluded, or referred to another provider.

What could change the final price or timeline?

Confirm how hidden damage, larger affected areas, difficult access, extra removal, disposal, emergency scheduling, or expanded scope would be priced and approved.
