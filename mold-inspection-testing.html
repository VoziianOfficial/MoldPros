Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting a mold inspection estimate.

Inspection and testing requests can include visual review, moisture checks, sampling, lab work, written reporting, and recommendations. These questions help make provider responses easier to compare.

What exactly is included in the inspection?

Ask whether the provider includes visual review, moisture readings, affected-area notes, attic or crawl space access, photos, written findings, or recommendations.

Will testing or sampling be recommended?

Confirm whether air samples, surface samples, bulk samples, or lab analysis are part of the estimate or priced separately.

How are results explained?

Ask whether the provider offers a written report, lab documentation, photos, moisture findings, and clear next-step guidance.

What could change the final price or timeline?

Confirm how extra rooms, difficult access, additional samples, lab turnaround time, emergency scheduling, or expanded scope would be priced and approved.
